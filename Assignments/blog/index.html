What Did Not Work?

I still had a hard time with fonts, and for some reason the hr tag at the bottom turned into a rectangle. I also struggled with using flexbox the way I wante to and had to end up changing it around as a bandaid solution. The page in general also just doesn't look the same as in the example and looks more unprofessional compared to it.


What Was Difficult to Reproduce?

The navigation felt difficult to reproduce since I don't know what they did for the spacing, and the text looks really small when I did it. I also had to keep adding IDs and classes to specific elements and tags in general, which was probably not a very efficient way to be doing this. I also had some trouble with the images until I realized I could have them align themselves, though I didn't know how to align them all at once correctly so I had to make a class with just two images so they'd be vertical while the other 3 would be horizontal. Overall, I wouldn't say most of this was easy for me or anything, since most of the parts that I did have an idea of how to do also ended up being botched in the end anyways, so the general aesthetics were still something difficult for me.
