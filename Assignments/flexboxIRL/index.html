How did you make the images feel consistent?

I manually changed their size in order to get them to be equal sizes. I also had to make sure they were aligned correctly so they'd feel consistent, which wasn't working at first. The thing that fixed it which I didn't expect was altering the size of the text boxes so they were equal sizes despite having less text. After that, it fixed the alignment issues I was having in general with the assignment and allowed the space-evenly property to work properly.


What happened when you resized the browser window?

The elements on the screen would get closer together, and the text would overlap with each other after the browser size gets smaller. I'm not completely sure what I could do in order to change the effects of resizing the browser window, like at least having the text not overlap with each other. I don't know what I can exactly do at the moment for the elements to not try to force themself onto the screen even when the browser is smaller, and I will probably have to learn how to fix that since people with devices that have smaller screens would have to deal with those issues.
